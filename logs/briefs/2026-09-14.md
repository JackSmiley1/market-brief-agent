## 1. Market Overview

Indices were modestly lower on the day — SPY -0.44%, QQQ -0.79%, IWM -0.35% — with the damage concentrated in AI-hardware/semis after Anthropic's CEO publicly called for slowing frontier AI development, spooking chip and data-center capex names. Simultaneously, traders are pricing in a >92% chance of a Fed rate hike this week, adding a macro overhang. Underneath the modest index-level decline was a sharp rotation: money moved out of AI infrastructure/hardware (NVDA, AMD, INTC, ORCL) and out of financials (BAC, GS) into mega-cap software/platform names (MSFT, GOOGL, META, CRM) and defensives.
