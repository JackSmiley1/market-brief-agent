## 1. Market Overview
Broad indices sold off Tuesday — SPY -0.68%, QQQ -1.26%, IWM -1.12% — driven primarily by a jump in the 10-year Treasury yield to its highest level since January 2025, compounded by fresh Middle East military escalation (new U.S. strikes on Iran) that spiked oil prices ~5%. This was a classic rotation day: energy and value/defensive names (XOM, CVX, JNJ, UNH) caught a bid while high-duration tech and richly-valued AI infrastructure names (ORCL, NVDA, AMD) got hit hardest. Apple was the notable outlier, rallying against the Nasdaq's decline on company-specific news.
