## 1. Market Overview
Major indices were essentially flat to slightly negative — SPY -0.02%, QQQ -0.29%, IWM -0.51% — masking a sharp internal rotation. Energy majors surged on oil-supply fears tied to the Strait of Hormuz, while semiconductors (NVDA, AMD, INTC) sold off hard, pulling the Nasdaq-heavy QQQ lower even as SPY held near flat. Overall tone: geopolitically-driven risk-off in growth/tech, risk-on in energy.
