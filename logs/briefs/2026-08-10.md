## 1. Market Overview
Major indices were essentially flat to slightly lower (SPY -0.02%, QQQ -0.29%, DIA -0.09%, IWM -0.51%), masking a sharp internal rotation. Crude oil spiked roughly 5% to over $82/barrel as hopes faded for a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz, which lifted energy stocks and simultaneously stoked inflation/rate-hike fears that pressured tech and semiconductors. Volume was generally light-to-average across the board (most volume ratios below 1.0), suggesting today's moves were driven by a specific macro headline rather than broad-based capitulation or accumulation.

## 4. Notable Events Today
- **Geopolitical/Macro**: Fading expectations for a Strait of Hormuz reopening deal drove crude oil up ~5% to over $82/barrel, the dominant macro theme of the day, per Reuters. This is inflationary and raises rate-hike concerns.
- **Intel capital raise**: $15B stock offering to fund contract chip manufacturing — dilutive and weighed on both INTC and general Nasdaq sentiment.
- **Nvidia financing story**: Reports (FT via Yahoo) of a $500B AI infrastructure financing consortium being assembled around Nvidia — a bullish long-term signal that did not translate into a bullish stock reaction today, worth monitoring for confirmation/details.
- **Apple downgrade**: Jefferies downgraded AAPL, cited in CNBC's Monday market-watch list.
- **Chevron/Microsoft deal**: 20-year "Project Kilby" gas power agreement for AI data centers — a structural, multi-year revenue stream for CVX tied to AI power demand.
- **Netflix**: Completed upfront ad sales with commitments nearly doubling YoY — reinforces the ad-tier growth narrative separate from subscriber growth.
- No major scheduled earnings or Fed data specifically noted for today, but Reuters/Yahoo pieces reference markets "bracing for key inflation data," implying a CPI or PCE release may be imminent this week.
