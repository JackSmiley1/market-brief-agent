## 6. Risk Notes

- **CPI inflation data** is the single biggest scheduled catalyst — a hot print could accelerate the yield/oil-driven selloff seen today and hit growth/tech names (QQQ, NVDA, AMD, AAPL) hardest; a cool print could quickly reverse today's tone.
- **Fed rate-hike expectations for next week** are explicitly tied to tomorrow's CPI in the news flow — this is a binary-ish event risk for the whole tape, not just tech.
- **Oil price volatility** (Brent ~$105, diesel >$6/gal) tied to escalating Middle East tensions (Iran/Houthi/sanctions) is a live macro wildcard that could keep energy names volatile and add cost-push inflation pressure feeding back into the rate story.
- **Earnings-reaction whiplash** (ORCL) is a reminder that even strong beats can sell off hard in this environment — good headline numbers are not being rewarded uniformly, which raises the bar for other upcoming tech earnings.
