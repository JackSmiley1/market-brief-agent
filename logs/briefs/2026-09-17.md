## 1. Market Overview

Broad rally across the board: SPY +1.14% to 762.64, QQQ +1.73% to 716.89, both recovering strongly the day after what CNBC described as a "Fed-induced" selloff. The Fed (under Chair Kevin Warsh) delivered its first rate hike in three years mid-week and reportedly said little else, which had pushed the 10-year yield to its highest level since 2007 and hit stocks Wednesday. Today's bounce looks like a broad relief/re-risking move, led disproportionately by semiconductors and mega-cap tech (QQQ outpacing SPY by 59bps), rather than a fundamental shift in the rate outlook.
