Methodology

Every nightly pick gets a self-rated confidence level and an event-risk flag from Claude's own read of the setup. Position size is cut for low confidence, scheduled-event risk, and short positions — not by assumption, but because each one was shown to correlate with worse real outcomes in this system's own trade history.

Before any comparison above is treated as a reason to change those sizing rules, it has to clear a minimum sample size (20 closed trades on both sides of the comparison). Buckets below that line are labeled small sample and are directional only — this is a deliberate guardrail against tuning the strategy on noise.

Lessons Learned

A separate, qualitative process (distinct from the sizing rules above) that periodically reviews batches of the system's own losing trades and writes down honest, falsifiable patterns — fed into future briefs as advisory context, not a hard rule. Only runs on a large-enough new batch of losses, and is explicitly allowed to conclude "no clear pattern."
